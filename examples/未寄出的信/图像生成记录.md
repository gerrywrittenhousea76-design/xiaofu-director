# 示例图生成记录

本示例使用S02实际参考图生成一张四格总览，查看后针对第4格交接完成状态做了一次局部修正。最终图片为 `分镜图.png`。不是四张独立生成的单镜原图，也没有生成动态视频或声音。

初始总览指令：

Use case: storyboard. Generate ONE finished cinematic storyboard overview image, EXACTLY FOUR panels in a 2 by 2 grid, read top-left, top-right, bottom-left, bottom-right. Each panel 16:9 and the overall canvas 16:9; thin neutral separations, NO captions, NO words, NO numbers, NO watermark. Use the attached reference for the same adult East Asian woman's identity, short black bob, cream knit cardigan over pale blouse and grey-green long skirt, and the natural cyan-green film palette. This is an original fictional scene, not a film recreation. All four panels take place in the same old open school corridor on the same afternoon: rice fields on LEFT open side, old wooden windows on RIGHT building wall, weathered grey-green narrow-mouth wastepaper bin against that right wall near the entrance. Keep soft side/backlight, gentle haze, fine film grain and a quiet restrained mood. The sealed unmarked ivory envelope stays in the woman's RIGHT hand until visibly transferred to the man's LEFT hand in the final panel; only ONE envelope exists.
PANEL 1 TOP LEFT: medium wide establishing view from the field side; woman on the left of composition, bin on right foreground. She looks down toward the bin and is raising her RIGHT hand holding the envelope toward its opening. Her fingers still grip it. Corridor and fields clearly establish the space. Only the woman visible.
PANEL 2 TOP RIGHT: tight insert of the woman's RIGHT hand in cream knit sleeve, still pinching the single sealed ivory envelope above the bin mouth. The letter has NOT been dropped. Frame hand, envelope and bin mouth clearly, quiet tension, soft muted old windows behind. Only one hand visible; anatomically correct right hand.
PANEL 3 BOTTOM LEFT: medium close view of same woman looking back down the corridor, as if she has just heard a familiar person; envelope withdrawn safely to her RIGHT waist and visible. Same left fields and right windows establish direction. The man is offscreen. No different clothes and no duplicate letter.
PANEL 4 BOTTOM RIGHT: medium two-shot from the same side: woman on LEFT and adult East Asian man on RIGHT, short black hair, plain navy cotton jacket over light grey shirt and dark trousers. They face each other. Show the specific completed handover clearly at frame center: the man has accepted the ONE sealed envelope with his LEFT hand, woman's RIGHT fingers have just opened away from it. No handshake, no embrace. Both faces and both hands visible. Same corridor and lighting, tender restrained faces. The woman has chosen to give the letter rather than throw it away.
Treat each panel as ONE decisive still, not multiple actions or a collage inside a panel. Preserve identity, wardrobe, environmental landmarks and envelope condition across all four. A polished readable 4-panel film storyboard.

局部修正：保留前三格和整体构图、人物、衣服、走廊、光线；仅将第4格改为来人持有信，女子的手松开且与信之间有可见间隙。
